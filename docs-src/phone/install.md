# Install the phone app

The phone app lets you answer sessions and get a ping when one needs you. It works on **iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later**.

Open **Settings → Phone app** on your computer. It shows a QR code and the steps.

![Phone app settings with QR code](shots/phone-install-1.png)

## Steps

1. **Open the link in Safari.** Scan the code with your iPhone camera, or type the address. It must be Safari.
2. **Tap Share.** It is the square with an arrow, in Safari's toolbar.
3. **Tap Add to Home Screen.** Scroll down the list if you don't see it, then tap **Add**.
4. **Open Hadji from your Home Screen.** Not from Safari. Notifications only reach the installed app.
5. **Tap Turn on notifications, then Allow.** You get a ping when a session needs you, or finishes one you follow.

![The Add to Home Screen step on iPhone](shots/phone-install-2.png)

## Your phones

When you finish step 5, your iPhone shows up under **Your phones**, with the date it was added. Until then it says **None yet.**

## If notifications are off

The page may say notifications are off on this Hadji, or that it does not send phone notifications yet. The app still installs.

## If you are already in Safari on the iPhone

Hadji shows a short prompt with just the steps left: Share, Add to Home Screen, then open it from the Home Screen. Dismiss it if you don't want it.
