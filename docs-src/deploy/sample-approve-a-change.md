## 1. Open the job that needs you

Jobs waiting for your OK show in **Needs you** on the left. Open one to see what Hadji did, check by check.

![The job page with its checklist and the changed code](sample-run.png)

## 2. Read what changed

The right side shows the exact lines changed. Green was added, red was removed. The checks above it say whether tests, types and review passed.

## 3. Approve, or say later

Tap **Approve** to ship. Tap **Later** to leave it waiting. Nothing ships until you choose. This works the same from your phone.

![The approval card on the phone](sample-approve.png)

> **Rather not click through it?** Ask Hadji: "ship the checkout fix". It shows you the same card and waits for your OK.
