# What Hadji is

Hadji is a workspace for building software with AI agents. You describe what you want. An agent does the work in its own safe space. You review it and decide what goes live.

## The pieces

- **Organization** — your team's space in Hadji. People, secrets and settings live here.
- **Project** — one GitHub repository that the Hadji app can see. Every repository you allow becomes a project.
- **Session** — one conversation with one agent about one piece of work. Each session gets its own sandbox: a private computer in the cloud, so it never touches your laptop.
- **Environment** — a place your app runs, like Preview or Production.

## What you do, what the agent does

- **You** say what you want, answer questions, and approve the risky steps.
- **The agent** reads the code, makes changes, runs checks, and opens a pull request on GitHub.
- **Hadji** shows you what is happening, and moves finished work toward your live site.

## The screens

- **Overview** — what a project is and where it stands.
- **Sessions** — where you talk to agents.
- **Issues** — your GitHub issues on a board.
- **Deploy** — your environments, and how a change moves from one to the next.
- **Files** — read and edit the project's files.
- **Settings** — people, secrets, models and more.
