# Pictures and handoffs

## Pictures

Sessions draw diagrams and screenshots to explain their work. The Overview keeps them.

- **Pinned** pictures sit in the right column of the [Overview](/docs/overview/project-overview/).
- **See all** opens this page, with every picture the project's sessions showed.

## Share a picture

1. Open a picture.
2. Choose to share it. This makes a public link.
3. Copy the link and send it.

Anyone with the link can open it without signing in. The Overview lists your public links, so you can find them again. If nothing is shared, it says "Nothing shared publicly yet." Your role has to allow sharing; Hadji checks this again when you share.

![Pictures page](https://hadji.ai/docs/media/shots/pictures-and-handoffs-1.png)

## Handoffs

A handoff is a note a session writes when it hands its work to a fresh start. It says what is done, what is left, and what to watch for. The Overview lists them in the right column. If there are none, it says "No handoffs yet."
