# Create an organization

An organization is your team's space in Hadji. You become its **owner**.

## Who can create one

Creating an organization is invite-only. You need an invite from the Hadji team, or you must be a platform admin. Without one, the page tells you so and shows the email you are signed in with.

## Steps

1. Open the organization switcher in the left rail and choose **New organization**.
2. Fill in the form and create it.
3. Connect GitHub next. See [Add a project](/docs/projects/add-project/).

![New organization form](https://hadji.ai/docs/media/shots/new-organization-1.png)

## After you create it

- Invite people in [Members and roles](/docs/settings/members-roles/).
- Add your name and logo in [Organization settings](/docs/settings/organization/).
