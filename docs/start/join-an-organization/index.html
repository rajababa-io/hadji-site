Sign in and join an organization

Sign in with Google and accept an invitation to join a team.

Updated Nov 2 · about 1 minutes

Sign in

  1. Open Hadji. You are sent to the sign-in page.
  2. Sign in with your Google account.

Accept an invitation

An admin invites you by email. The email holds a link.

  1. Open the link while signed in. Opening it accepts it. If you are signed out, you sign in first and then land on the invitation.
  2. You join the organization with the role the admin chose. See Members and roles.

If it does not work

  • No email arrived — ask the admin to check the invitation. On some installs email is held, so the admin has to send you the link themselves.
  • The link does not work — ask the admin to cancel the old invitation and send a new one.
  • Company domain — some organizations let anyone with a company email join by themselves.

After you join

Connect your own GitHub account in Connected accounts, so sessions can open pull requests as you.
