# Session instructions

Instructions shape how sessions write to people and work with them.

There are two layers:

- **Workspace** — **Settings → Session instructions**. Everyone in the organization. Admins change it. It starts from Hadji's default. Add to it, or replace it.
- **Yours** — **Settings → Your instructions**. Only you, and only your sessions follow them. They start from what the workspace gives.

![Session instructions](https://hadji.ai/docs/media/shots/instructions-1.png)

## On the same pages

- **Reply checks** — checks a session's reply goes through.
- **Default skills** (workspace page) — skills every session starts with.

## Tips

- Keep them short and clear.
- Say what you want, not what you don't.
- If a session behaves oddly, read the workspace instructions first.
