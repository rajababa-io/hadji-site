Notifications

Get a desktop alert when a session needs you.

Updated Nov 2 · about 1 minutes

Open Settings → Notifications. This is for this browser only. Nothing is stored on the server.

  1. Turn on session notifications.
  2. Your browser asks for permission. Click Allow.

If it does not work

  • "This browser cannot show notifications here." — use a browser that supports them.
  • "This browser has blocked notifications for this site." — allow them in the browser's site settings.

On your phone

Desktop alerts do not reach your phone. See Install the phone app.
