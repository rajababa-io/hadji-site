# Your profile

Open **Settings → Profile**. It is about you, and it is the same in every organization you belong to.

![Profile settings](https://hadji.ai/docs/media/shots/profile-1.png)

## What you can change

- **Name** — first and last.
- **Photo.**
- **Time zone.** Times in the console use it.
- **Sign out** of this browser.

## Not here

- Your GitHub, Claude and Codex accounts are in [Connected accounts](/docs/settings/connected-accounts/).
- Notifications are in [Notifications](/docs/settings/notifications/).
