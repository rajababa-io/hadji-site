# Preferences

Settings open as a window over the page you are on. Open it from the settings button in the left rail. It starts on **Preferences**.

The menu has three groups, so you can see who a change reaches:

- **You** — only you.
- **Workspace** — everyone in the organization.
- **Projects** — everyone on that project.

![Settings window, Preferences](https://hadji.ai/docs/media/shots/preferences-1.png)

## What you can change

- **Interface theme** — every theme has a light and a dark side.
- **Mode** — light, dark, or System, which follows your device.
- **Accent color** — yours on every device and in every org. Without one, your organization's color is used.
- **Text size** — scales the whole interface.
- **Time format** — how timestamps on messages show.
- **Tip of the day** — one short lesson about once a week.

These are saved for this browser, except the accent color, which follows you.
