Audit log

See who did what in the organization, newest first.

Updated Nov 2 · about 1 minutes

Open Settings → Audit log under Workspace. Only owners and admins see it.

What it shows

Who did what, newest first. It includes changes made in the app and by the engine. Times show in your time zone.

If nothing has happened yet, it says Nothing has happened yet.

Good to know

  • Revealing a secret is recorded. See Secrets.
  • People who have left the organization still show.
