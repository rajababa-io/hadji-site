Members and roles

Invite people, set what each role can do, and lower a role on one project.

Updated Nov 2 · about 2 minutes

Open Settings → Members under Workspace. Admins and owners see it.

Roles in the organization

  • Owner — everything, including billing, handing ownership to someone else, and deleting the organization.
  • Admin — people, settings, GitHub, secrets, the audit log, and hiding projects.
  • Member — uses every project the org shows, starts work and sessions, and can see who is in the org.

Each person's role sets the highest role they can have on any project.

Invite someone

  1. Type their email in Invite by email.
  2. Choose member or admin. If members may invite, the choice is not offered and they invite members only.
  3. Send. Hadji emails them a link. See Sign in and join an organization.

If email is held on your install, the page says they were not emailed. Send them the link yourself. You can cancel an invitation, and send a new one.

Other controls on the page

  • Change a role or remove a person.
  • Who can invite — admins only, or everyone. When everyone, members can invite people, as members.
  • Join by company domain — let anyone with your company email join.
  • Agents — an agent calls Hadji with a token, not a sign-in. Admins run these.
  • What each role can do — a list, with your role marked.
  • Hand over ownership — owners only. The org must keep an owner.

Lower a role on one project

A project admin can lower someone's role on one project, never raise it. For example, a member can be set to Viewer on a project.

  1. Open Settings, then the project under Projects.
  2. Open People.
  3. Change the person's role.

A Viewer can read everything on the project, but cannot send messages, edit files or start work.

What members cannot do

Changing roles, removing people and cancelling invitations is never opened to members.
