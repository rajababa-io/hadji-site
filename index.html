The problem

Agents can do real work now. Keeping up with them is the hard part.

"What is it doing?"

Agents work in windows you never see. Progress lives in one person's head.

"Who said it could?"

Nobody signs off before something important ships, merges or gets sent.

"I can't read this."

Updates come back as walls of logs and code. You need the decision, not the details.

How it works

Same steps, every time.

Hadji turns the way your team already works into a path agents follow — with you in the loop where it counts.

Say what you need

In plain words, or pick a ready-made path: fix this, build that, check this.

Hadji does the work

Plan, build, check, review — each step in its own walled-off workspace.

You approve what matters

It stops where you choose, and shows you the choice as a picture.

It's all written down

Every step, approval and result is kept, so you always know who did what.
