The problem

Agents can do real work now. Keeping track of them is the hard part.

"What is it doing right now?"

Agents run in terminals and chat windows you can't see. Progress lives in someone else's head.

"Who said it could do that?"

There's no point where a person signs off before something important ships, merges or gets sent.

"I can't read any of this."

Updates come back as walls of logs and code. Managers need the decision, not the diff.

How it works

Same steps, every time.

Hadji turns the way your team already works into a repeatable path that agents follow — with you in the loop where it counts.

Describe the work

Ask in plain words, or pick a ready-made path: fix this, build that, review this.

Agents do it, step by step

Plan, build, check, review — each step in its own walled-off workspace.

You approve what matters

The run pauses at the gates you choose and shows you the decision as a picture.

Everything is on the record

Every action, approval and result is logged, so you always know who did what.

What you get

Built for the person in charge.

Updates you can read

Short, plain-English replies written for a manager — what happened, what's next, what needs you.

Decisions as pictures

Options laid out side by side in a diagram, so choosing takes seconds, not a meeting.

Approval gates

Nothing important goes out until a person says yes. You decide where the gates sit.

One screen for every agent

Every run, every project, every agent — what's working, what's waiting, what's done.

Your keys, your data

Bring your own AI provider keys. Each company's people, files and secrets are kept apart.

A full audit trail

Who started it, who approved it, what changed. Ready when someone asks.
